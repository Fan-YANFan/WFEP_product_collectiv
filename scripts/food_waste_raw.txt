環境保護署在全港不同地區設立了 105 個「廚
餘回收流動點 」，每晚以定時定點街站 或流動
車的方式， 為附近小型商戶和居民提供廚餘回
收服務。 
 
The Environmental Protection Department has set 
up 105 Food Waste Recycling Spots at different 
districts across Hong Kong.  These recycling spots 
operate as kerbside booths or mobile truck at fixed 
hours and locations every night to provide food 
waste recycling service for nearby small business 
operators and residents.
